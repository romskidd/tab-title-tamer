# Tab Title Tamer

**Your tabs, on your terms.** Freeze tab titles, hold or replace tab icons, and
strip notification-count clutter like `(3)` or `(1000+)` — with precise
per-site rules.

  <a href="https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tab-title-tamer/goegcjnemodnihadjcfgaodbgnhpomhj"><b>Install for Chrome</b></a>

</p>

## What it does

- **Freeze a tab's title** so the page can't keep rewriting it — always, or
  only while the tab is inactive.
- **Strip notification counts** from titles: `(3)`, `(12)`, `(1000+)`, `(5M+)`…
  Real parentheses are left alone.
- **Hold a site's tab icon** so badges and red dots can't change it — free for
  every site.
- **Replace icons entirely**: coloured letter monograms are free; emoji, your
  own image, or an icon borrowed from another open tab with Pro.
- **Custom tab titles** — three sites free, unlimited with Pro.
- **Precise per-site rules**: bare domain or a single subdomain free; every
  subdomain (`*.site.com`), patterns (`dev-*`) and paths (`site.com/inbox`)
  with Pro. The most specific rule wins.
- **Live before/after preview** in the popup, automatic modes, backup &
  restore, light and dark themes.

Pro comes with a **7-day free trial**, no payment method required.
