# Tab Title Tamer

**Your tabs, on your terms.** Tame tab titles, freeze or replace tab icons,
mark lookalike tabs with a color, and strip notification-count clutter like
`(3)` or `(1000+)`, with precise per-site rules.

## What it does

- **Tame a tab's title** so the page can't keep rewriting it. Always, or only
  while the tab is inactive.
- **Strip notification counts** from titles: `(3)`, `(12)`, `(1000+)`, `(5M+)`…
  Real parentheses are left alone.
- **Freeze a site's tab icon** so badges and red dots can't change it. Free for
  every site.
- **Mark tabs that look alike** with a color: a band, a corner, a dot or a ring
  drawn over the site's own icon. Your work inbox and your personal one, one
  client and another, dev and production. Red, green and orange are free.
- **Title markers**: wrap the live title with your own prefix or suffix,
  `[PROD] Dashboard`, without taming it.
- **Quick styles**: save your favorite markers and apply one in a single tap
  from the popup. Three free, sixteen with Pro.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: tame this tab (`Alt+Shift+T`), freeze its icon
  (`Alt+Shift+I`), cycle its marker (`Alt+Shift+B`). They create the rule for
  you if the site has none.
- **Replace icons entirely**: colored letter monograms are free; emoji, your own
  image, or an icon borrowed from another open tab with Pro.
- **Custom tab titles**: three sites free, unlimited with Pro.
- **Precise per-site rules**: bare domain or a single subdomain free; every
  subdomain (`*.site.com`), patterns (`dev-*`) and paths (`site.com/inbox`)
  with Pro. The most specific rule wins.
- **Ports and IPs are part of a rule**: `localhost:3000` and `localhost:8080`
  are two different sites, and both are free.
- **Pause a rule** instead of deleting it: everything kept, nothing applied, one
  click to bring it back.
- **Paste a whole list** of addresses and get every rule at once.
- **A guided setup on install** that offers the sites you already have open.
- Live before/after preview in the popup, automatic modes, backup & restore,
  light and dark themes.

## Languages

English, French, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. It follows your
browser, or you pick one in the sidebar.

Pro comes with a **7-day free trial**, no payment method required.
